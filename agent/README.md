# Local job agent through Step 3

The existing launchd job scans every 15 minutes, deduplicates URLs, checks availability, and saves full job descriptions. Records remain in collection order. No fit ratings, rankings, or hosted-model calls are used.

Step 3 applies deterministic eligibility rules before inference. Clear non-engineering titles, explicit senior titles, mandatory 5+ years of experience, explicit citizenship restrictions or active-clearance requirements, and explicit engineering team leadership duties are rejected with verbatim evidence. Preferred requirements, alternative experience paths, three/four-year bars, generic export-control language, and ambiguous leadership language are flagged. Company history and negated clearance requirements are excluded. Unclear cases proceed. Rules are tested in test_screening.py; they are conservative text rules, not a guarantee of perfect interpretation.

For passing jobs, the existing H-1B plugin reads the local DOL index. Employer names must match after punctuation/legal-suffix normalization. Different or uncertain names remain unknown. H-1B LCA filings, certified filings, PERM filings, years and lookup time/source are retained. Null counts remain unknown. Historical filings do not promise future sponsorship. This lookup never affects rejection. Lookup errors are informational and do not stop matching.

Nemotron `nemotron-3.5-bolt:e4b-nvfp4` matches JD requirements to canonical cv.md facts. Source IDs are validated. Screening reasons/flags and H-1B history appear in the state, evidence report, dashboard and refine prompt. Rejected jobs stay visible, with their exact rejection reason; they are not repeatedly processed. Each successful passing job becomes local_ready, the handoff after Step 3. Resume Matcher now builds a one-page Word draft after both screening stages pass; local_ready requires a verified draft.

Each scan drains the full queue sequentially with a two-second pause between attempts. JDs are fetched fresh, H-1B lookups use a fresh temporary result cache, and evidence inference never reads saved results. Evidence logs remain on disk for audit only. Every completed passing packet qualifies for an alert from 6am to midnight, with overnight packets announced after 6am. Failed jobs retry at the end of the queue, up to three attempts, then remain visible as errors. The dashboard refreshes after every processed job. The dashboard shows Collected, Rejected, Pending and Evidence ready totals. Rejected jobs appear separately with supporting evidence and no Apply or Refine buttons. Refine prompts remain available for non-rejected jobs. Evidence-ready means Step 3 is complete, not that a resume has been built.

Tests: python3 local/agent/test_local.py and python3 local/agent/test_screening.py.

All local jobs-workflow inference defaults to this same Nemotron model, including the separate Resume Matcher adapter. No automatic alternate-model or cloud fallback. Ollama is configured for one loaded model and one request at a time. The adapter runs sequentially inside the live cycle. Builds use fresh output directories, preserve a source ledger and render verification PDF, and require manual final polish.

JobSpy discovery is enabled for Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor and ZipRecruiter. Each board runs SWE, AI Engineer and FDE searches for San Francisco, CA with a 48-hour window and 10 requested results per query. Board calls are serialized with bounded worker timeouts. Partial failures are logged and included in last_scan.errors while successful results continue. The existing scanner filters and deduplication apply before queue insertion. Screening is unchanged.
